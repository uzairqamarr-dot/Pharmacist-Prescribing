Antihypertensive therapies and treatment targets

Record your notes below

**\
When is antihypertensive treatment is recommended?**\
For those at high CVD risk, drug treatment may be considered in addition
to lifestyle advice.\
\
• In those with identified masked hypertension, drug therapy and
lifestyle advice is recommended. These patients are reported to have
increased CVD risk

**\
How does cardiovascular risk influence treatment decisions?**\
CVD risk determines the speed of therapy initiation. High risk of CVD
(\>15%) to begin treatment immediately sometimes without needing to use
CVD risk calculator due to present individual risk factors.\
\
low risk = \<10% - review based on BP readings\
moderate =10-15% - Review as long as individual risk factors and BP are
ruled out

**\
What are the recommended blood pressure targets?**\
Normal ≤ 130/85\
\
Adults with uncomplicated hypertension \< 140/90 (lower if tolerated)\
\
Adults with hypertension \> 75 years without diabetes SBP \< 120\
\
In pregnancy \< 135/85\
\
Hypertension with comorbidities or end organ damage e.g. coronary heart
disease, diabetes, chronic kidney disease, stroke or TIA, COPD \<
130/80\
\
Those with proteinuria \> 1 g per day (with or without diabetes) \<
125/75\
\
Children \> 10 years of age with a BMI \> 85th centile for age and
gender\
\
See Special considerations (child)

**\
In which situations are more intensive treatments appropriate?**\
when previous therapies do not work and you have to add more agents
which also do not work after sometime spent.
