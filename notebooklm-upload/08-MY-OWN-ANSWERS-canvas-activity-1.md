What systolic and diastolic values indicate Grade 1, 2 and 3
hypertension?**\

Grade 2: 160-179/100-109\
Grade 3:\>180/\>110

When should home or ambulatory blood pressure monitoring be
considered?**\
When patients clinic blood pressure is \>140/90 or hypertension is
suspected

vs hypertensive urgency situation?**\
Hypertensive emergency: \>220/140\
