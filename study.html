Scope — read this once. PHAR6302 states directly that NSW expanded scope does not include routine pharmacist management or prescribing for hypertension. The Queensland pilot protocols in here are taught as an example of a pharmacist-led model of care, not as your scope of practice. Use them to learn assessment, referral pathways and emerging models — not as a licence. Your course also directs you to the Heart Foundation guideline (2016) and the Australian CVD risk calculator as primary references, and to the Australian Asthma Handbook over Therapeutic Guidelines where they differ.

How this works

Cards sit in five boxes. Get one right and it moves up a box and comes back less often; get it wrong and it drops to box 1 and returns immediately. Box 5 cards resurface rarely. That means your review time concentrates on what you don't know, which is the whole point — flipping through cards you already know feels productive and isn't.
