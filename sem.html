              What each one actually involves

              Prescribing Portfolio + WBAs 6202

              Evidence of learning across the semester: clinical case reflections, prescribing activities, documentation of clinical experiences, self-assessment, supervisor feedback. Must reflect on culturally safe prescribing incl. care for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples. WBAs are clinical tasks done in practice — history taking, physical exam, diagnostic planning, management plans, documentation. No AI permitted.

              GenAI Reflection 6202

              12-minute pre-recorded video of a simulated patient consultation. You document your own clinical notes, then compare them against notes produced by an approved GenAI tool. Budget filming and editing time on top of the writing.

              Multi-station OSCE 6202

              Several stations covering acute presentations and chronic/complex cases. Assesses history taking, examination, interpretation of investigations, clinical reasoning, diagnosis, management, communication, person-centred care. On campus. Cut-score by borderline regression. Supplementary attempt available but caps the course mark at 50.

              Action Plan + Viva Voce 6302

              Written chronic condition action plan relevant to your scope of practice, submitted Week 6. It then forms the basis of an oral examination with assessors in Week 10. Reattempt possible on either component.

              Interprofessional Handover 6302

              Written ISBAR handover for a chronic condition case, plus critical reflection. AI allowed for drafting and expression only — never the clinical content.

              The portfolio is the single easiest thing to fall behind on. It's due 13 Nov but built from evidence gathered all semester — you can't retrofit twelve weeks of clinical reflection in the final fortnight. One entry a week keeps it honest.
              The portfolio must include reflection on culturally safe prescribing practice, covering cultural, social and health system factors — including care provided to Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples and other priority populations. Log at least a few entries under that category as you go.
              WIL participation needs sign-off by your DPP to count. Keep the log current so there are no gaps to reconstruct at the end.
