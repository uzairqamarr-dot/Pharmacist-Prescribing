# NotebookLM setup — how to use the upload bundle

Everything is in the **`notebooklm-upload/`** folder. Nothing was moved or
deleted from anywhere else — those are copies, and the app and your original
files are untouched.

## What to do

1. Open **notebooklm.google.com** → **Create new** → name it
   `PHAR6202 + PHAR6302 — Sem 2 2026`.
2. Drag in **every file from `notebooklm-upload/` except the one starting
   `00-`**. That's 19 sources.
3. Open `00-PASTE-THIS-IN-CHAT-do-not-upload.md` and paste its blocks into the
   chat, in order. Paste 1 first, always.

## Why the filenames look like that

NotebookLM cites by source name, so the number prefix shows up inside the
citation itself. `07-UNVERIFIED-classmate-Wk3-4-study-notes` in a footnote tells
you instantly not to trust that number without checking. The tiers:

| Prefix | Tier |
|---|---|
| `01-COURSE-CONTEXT-AND-DATES` | Context I wrote — dates, scope, trust rules. Not a clinical source |
| `02-PROTOCOL-QLD-*` | Primary — Queensland pilot protocols |
| `03-GUIDELINE-HeartFoundation-*` | Primary — but a *different framework* |
| `04-COURSE-*` | Primary for what's assessed |
| `06-TUTORIAL-transcript-*` | A tutor teaching, not policy |
| `07-UNVERIFIED-classmate-*` | One student's interpretation |
| `08-MY-OWN-ANSWERS-*` | Your Canvas answers — contain known errors |

## Three things worth knowing

**The Canvas exports aren't module content — they're your own answers.** I
opened them: they're the questions with your typed responses underneath. Two of
those responses are wrong (`<130/80` for comorbidities, `<125/75` for
proteinuria). Uploaded as-is, NotebookLM would have cited your own mistakes back
to you as course material. Each one now carries a header saying what it is, and
Paste 8 turns them into a marking exercise instead — which is the single most
useful thing in the bundle.

**The `.docx` files wouldn't have uploaded.** NotebookLM takes PDF, txt,
Markdown, Google Docs and URLs — not Word. Converted to Markdown.

**The CKD case study is deliberately excluded.** De-identified real patient,
small region, your classmate flagged the re-identification risk themselves.
Don't put it in a cloud notebook.

## Verify before you trust it

Paste 2 is a trap question with a known answer: at 148/92 with 7% five-year
risk, the Queensland protocol and the Heart Foundation **agree** — neither
starts a drug. If NotebookLM says the Heart Foundation would treat, it's
carrying a risk-band label across frameworks and its comparisons aren't
reliable until you re-paste rule 4.
