## Two Handbook facts worth using on any prospective DPP

- **WIL needn't be in business hours.** Evenings and weekends are explicitly allowed.
- **It needn't be full days.** Short sessions, case discussions and reviewing
  consultation write-ups all count. (Not counted: independent study, reading alone,
  documentation without supervision, admin, travel time.)

"Three hours a week, whenever suits, including after hours" is a far easier yes than
"30 hours".
